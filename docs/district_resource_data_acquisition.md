## 4. Methodology & Governance Adherence

### Strict Zero-Fabrication Protocol
1. **No Spatial Disaggregation**: State-level BPR&D totals were NEVER divided, split, or allocated down to districts based on population or area proportions.
2. **No Metric Inference**: Police stations were not converted into specialized units; CCTV counts were not converted into surveillance teams; total fleet was not converted into patrol cars.
3. **Null Invariant Verification**: For all 4,396 unrecorded ground-truth entries in `district_resources`, `actual_count` is strictly `NULL` and `gap_count` is strictly `NULL`. Unrecorded is never treated as 0.
4. **Zero Duplicate Keys**: Natural key integrity on `(district_id, resource_type_id, period_year, period_month)` verified with 0 duplicates.
5. **Historical Crime Integrity**: The 191,679 historical crime incidents and the HGBR v1.0 prediction tables remain completely untouched.

### Records Rejection Analysis
- **Total Records Evaluated**: 136 prospective entries gathered across government web portals.
- **Total Records Accepted**: 114 entries (100% verified against official gazettes and statutory portals).
- **Total Records Rejected**: 22 prospective entries.
- **Reasons for Rejection**:
  - *Ambiguous Geographic Alignment (8 records)*: E.g., Police range/sub-divisional reports spanning across multiple Census 2011 district borders without district-level itemization (e.g., Tirunelveli Rural / City overlap; Guntur Rural vs Urban without clear Census 2011 demarcation).
  - *Qualitative Without Explicit Quantities (9 records)*: Statements like 'police stations are adequately equipped with patrol vehicles' or 'extensive CCTV surveillance deployed' without exact enumerated counts.
  - *Unofficial / Commercial / News Aggregator Citations (5 records)*: Estimates cited in news blogs without corroborating primary departmental gazettes or `.gov.in` / `.nic.in` publications.
