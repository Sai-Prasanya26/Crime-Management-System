## Compliance & Governance Verification

- **Zero AI Generation:** Verified that no AI-generated, synthetic, Midjourney, DALL-E, Gemini, or Stable Diffusion images are present. Every photograph originates from real human photography with verified public provenance.
- **Local Storage:** All assets are stored locally in the repository under `frontend/public/images/` and `frontend/public/assets/images/`. The frontend does not hotlink or make external runtime requests for homepage imagery.
- **License Compliance:** All images are sourced under the permissive Pexels License (free commercial and non-commercial reuse) with complete author attribution and exact source URLs.
