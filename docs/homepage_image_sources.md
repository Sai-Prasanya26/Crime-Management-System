## Compliance & Governance Verification

- **Zero AI Generation:** Verified that no AI-generated, synthetic, Midjourney, DALL-E, Gemini, or Stable Diffusion images are present.
- **Local Storage:** All assets are stored locally in the repository under `frontend/public/images/`. The frontend does not make external runtime requests for homepage imagery.
- **License Compliance:** All images are sourced under either the permissive Pexels License (free commercial and non-commercial reuse) or Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike licenses (CC BY-SA 3.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0) with complete author attribution.
