# Photographic Asset Registry & Image Sources

This document provides complete provenance, source URLs, licensing, and attribution details for all photographic assets used in the **Crime Intelligence & Management Portal** (`Data-Driven Crime Management System with AI-Based Resource Optimization`).

---

## Provenance & Compliance Standards

1. **Zero Synthetic / AI Generation**: Every asset in this registry is a real-world law enforcement, emergency management, or public-safety photograph captured by verified organizations and photographers. No generative AI or synthetic imaging tools were used.
2. **Open & Permissive Licensing**: All assets are sourced from verified public domain repositories or under Creative Commons licenses (CC0, CC BY 2.0, CC BY 4.0, CC BY-SA 4.0).
3. **No Impersonation or Government Emblems**: No photographs contain official emblems of the Government of India (such as the State Emblem / Lion Capital of Ashoka), official ministry seals, or misleading representations of official national ownership. The platform is an academic/operational decision-support system.
4. **Optimized for Web Performance**: All original high-resolution master captures have been scaled using high-fidelity Lanczos resampling to web-appropriate dimensions (1067px–1500px) and quality-compressed (100KB–233KB) to provide immediate page loads and crisp UI rendering.
